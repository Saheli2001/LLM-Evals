## Failure cases

- Unfaithful responses
- Answer relevance
- correctness, citation etc.

## Metrics

- Faithfulness. It dosent mean correctness. It means that the answer is generated by the retrieved context only.

- Answer relevance. 

## How to calculate the metrices

1. Dataset: col1: question, col2: golden context.
2. The generator gets the question and context and the generator produces and output. The answer is provided in LLM-as-a-judge and it will break it into claims and it will check whether the claims are in the golden context. 

faithfulness = total number of claims that are in golden context/ total number of claims.

3. Answer relevance is reference free eval. LLM as a judge takes the question and generator output as its input. Breaks the output as claims and checks if each claim is helping to generate the a relevant answer.
